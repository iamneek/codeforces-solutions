so while solving this problem, I learned that for any sum that can be formed by k numbers within range n,
the sum will always be within the minimum and maximum sum of k numbers from n.


min sum is the 1...k sum
max sum is the n-k...n sum

if a sum x can be formed using k numbers within 1 to n numbers then the sum x should be:

min sum >= x >= max sum

here's an example:

n := 5, k := 3, x := 10

1, 2, 3, 4, 5

min sum: 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 (upto 3 cuz k = 3)
max sum: 3 + 4 + 5 = 12

WHY?

there's a pattern:
if we consider the possible set of k combinations:

1 + 2 + 3 = 6 (the min sum we got before)

1 + 2 + 4 = 7

1 + 2 + 5 = 8 (max we can go is up to 5 because n = 5)

1 + 3 + 5 = 9 (increase the second place for new permutation)

1 + 4 + 5 = 10 (max is 4 for second place, because the question says sum of DISTINCT numbers, so can't be 5 + 5 + 5 or something repetitive.)

2 + 4 + 5 = 11

3 + 4 + 5 = 12 (the max sum we got before)



if we look at the output, we can see that the output is increment of 1 from min sum to max sum 

so if x can be formed with k, it will 100% be between min and max sum obtained from k numbers in n range,
because the sums within min sum and max sum is in increasing order.
